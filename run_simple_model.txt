PART 3  STRESS TEST ON DELIBERATELY NASTY ECONOMIES
==============================================================================
  Every feature that could feed the one channel the simplification does NOT
  remove -- profits earned abroad and repatriated -- is switched on:
  scrambled cross-ownership, countries with no local production, extreme
  size asymmetry, extreme elasticities, tariffs.


  Split by whether (H1) -- no group above half of any market -- holds AT THE
  POINT. That is the same condition Theorem 2 already needs, and it is what
  Theorem 5 assumes; a stress point that violates it is outside both theorems.

  READING. The simplification removes the channel that is peculiar to this
  model and provably signs the reallocation block. What survives is the
  ordinary income effect through repatriated profits, which is common to
  every model with non-labour income. It is measured, not assumed away.
