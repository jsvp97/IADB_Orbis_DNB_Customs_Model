PART 1  WHEN DOES A GROUP'S WAGE BILL RISE WITH ITS MARKET SHARE?
==========================================================================
  Bill proportional to S/mu(S); rises iff eps_mu(S) < 1.
  conduct    sigma  eta    threshold     entry frontier (Thm 2)
  cournot    5.0    1.0    0.501         0.548
  cournot    5.0    1.5    0.858         0.936
  cournot    8.0    1.0    0.501         0.532
  cournot    3.0    1.0    0.501         0.572
  bertrand   5.0    1.0    0.691         0.680
  bertrand   5.0    1.5    0.790         0.776
  bertrand   8.0    1.0    0.739         0.708
  bertrand   3.0    1.0    0.634         0.660

  Closed form, Cournot eta=1: mu = sigma/[(sigma-1)(1-S)], so eps_mu = S/(1-S),
  which is < 1 exactly when S < 1/2. THE SAME ONE-HALF THRESHOLD AS THEOREM 2.
