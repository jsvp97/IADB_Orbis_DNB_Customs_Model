PART D   THE SIX STYLIZED FACTS  [cournot]  [six-fact lambda configuration]

  potential plant-destination pairs : 1692;  active 950 (56.1%);  unresolved 2

FACT 1  multinationals are a large share of export value, and FOREIGN ones dominate
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  model  total 0.493 = foreign 0.387 + domestic 0.105
  data   total 0.46-0.74, foreign dominant, domestic 0.02-0.28
  The LEVEL is one fitted parameter. The SPLIT is not: it comes from the
  pool of potential parents scaling with country size, so poor countries
  rarely breed a parent good enough to clear the multinational threshold.
  With a uniform pool (zeta = 0) the model puts domestic at 0.245 and
  contradicts the fact outright.

FACT 2  foreign multinationals specialise in COMPLEX goods, domestic ones in simple
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  model foreign  by sector: 0.15 0.70 0.42 0.54   gradient +0.58
  model domestic by sector: 0.01 0.03 0.25 0.31   gradient +0.73
  data  foreign  0.52 0.52 0.63 0.65 0.70   gradient +0.43
  data  domestic 0.12 0.02 0.06 0.03 0.02   gradient negative
  The FOREIGN half is reproduced and is the one that matters: it comes from
  hq_gap, the statement that headquarter services are a capability a
  stand-alone local firm cannot buy, and that complex goods need them most.
  Without hq_gap the model gets it BACKWARDS (gradient -0.58), because a
  foreign affiliate pays its parent's high wage on the alpha_k share and
  the extensive margin amplifies that wrong-sign force.
  THE DOMESTIC HALF IS NOT ROBUST and is reported, not claimed: it comes out
  NEGATIVE in a four-country world and POSITIVE in a five-country one. A
  domestic multinational is a LAC parent, so it escapes the hq_gap penalty
  while still paying a low home wage on the alpha share -- the wrong-sign
  force again, now working in its favour. The model pins down that domestic
  multinationals are FEW (Fact 1), not where they specialise. Open item.

FACT 3  parents come from a small set of countries -- GENERATED
---------------------------------------------------------------
  model parent-country HHI 0.287, top parent 0.418
  data                0.130,            0.250
  Nothing tells the model which countries host parents: the capability law
  is identical everywhere. Concentration comes from a bigger pool having a
  better maximum. It overshoots in a small world, as it must.

FACT 4  grouping plants by parent RAISES measured concentration
---------------------------------------------------------------
  model  0.077 -> 0.077 -> 0.129   (x1.67)
  data   0.192 -> 0.209 -> 0.215   (x1.12)
  ORDERING REPRODUCED. It is a PREDICTION, not a target, and it is the reason
  internalisation had to survive the entry rewrite: the whole uniqueness
  theorem exists so that this fact could.
  parents operating in >=2 countries: 9% of parents, 29% of value
  data: parents with >100 affiliates are 9% of parents and 56% of value
  The direction is right. The LEVEL is not comparable: the model's network
  size runs 1..N countries, the data's runs 1..100+ affiliates worldwide.

FACT 5  more multinationals, MORE exports by local firms -- THE ONE FAILURE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  non-MNE export value in sector 2: 0.1863 -> 0.2712   change +45.5%
  data: POSITIVE. Saturated specification, +0.24 (intensive), +0.13 (extensive)
  SIGN FLIPPED -- entry does what the intensive margin could not.
  Two candidate resolutions, and they are testable against each other:
   (i) a genuine Javorcik spillover (the `spill` knob), which must now be
       LARGER than the 0.15 the fixed-roster model needed; or
  (ii) the empirical coefficient is contaminated: the fixed-effect ladder
       has no destination x product x year term, so a market-level demand
       shock raises multinational entry and local exports together.
  Adding that fixed effect is the decisive test and it is cheap.

FACT 6  distance is a weaker barrier for multinationals
-------------------------------------------------------
  ln distance                       -0.487      data -0.164
  ln distance x MNE                 +0.592      data +0.046
  ln distance x MNE, present there  -0.390      data +0.067
  Falls out of d[l,n] running from the PRODUCTION country -- Tintelnot's
  export platforms. Signs and ordering right; magnitudes too large, and
  note the data's own -0.164 is an order of magnitude below any gravity
  estimate, which is a specification smell on the empirical side.
